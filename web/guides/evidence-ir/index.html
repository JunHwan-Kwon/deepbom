The five members

RepresentationWhat it records
Artifact IRObserved artifact structure, native facts and serialized storage.

Activation IR · optionalImported execution captures bound to a model, run and probe. Static inspection does not create runtime measurements.
Provenance IR · optionalConnections to datasets, code, runs, reports and external BOMs; supplied-file consistency and declared relationships remain distinct.

Shared rules

Artifact IR is the source for Model IR. Optional members bind to that model without modifying its identity. References, digests, exact counts and coverage must remain consistent. Unknown, unsupported and unassessed values stay explicit.

The family JSON Schema accepts one member document. It is not a bundle format. JSON Schema checks structure; member validators additionally check source bindings and semantic consistency. A digest alone does not authenticate a publisher or prove a lineage claim.

These names describe a DEEPBOM-maintained specification. They do not imply endorsement or certification by OMOP, CycloneDX, SPDX or W3C. External mappings retain their source standards and limits.

Current contract and transition

Provenance IR uses deepbom.provenance_ir.v1, provenance_ir_sha256, and the sole --section provenance_ir selector. Retired names are rejected. Regenerate evidence from the model, a current metadata template and supporting files; do not relabel an old hashed document.

The 2.0.0 release introduces this breaking transition across web assets, CLI and remote MCP validators. Read the transition rules.

Exchange through TEA

TEA describes discovery and exchange of transparency documents. It can deliver future exports while Evidence IR continues to define and validate their evidence. A TEA adapter is proposed, not implemented. Read the pinned specification review.
